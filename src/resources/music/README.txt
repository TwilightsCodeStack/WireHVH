Put your MP3 music files in this folder.

In W1RE, open Music > Refresh tracks, select a file, and click Play.
The default music folder is resources\music beside W1RE.dll.
You can also use Load path on the Music page with a full MP3 or folder path.
Embedded title, artist, album, and cover art are displayed when present.
Music keeps playing when Insert hides the interface; End stops it on unload.
